# U.S. County Atlas

Interactive map of all 3,142 U.S. counties. Color the map by any stat, stack filters, and tap a county for its full fact sheet. Works on desktop and iPhone.

**Layers:** race & ethnicity, Muslim % (state level), 2024 violent & property crime, take-home pay on a $100k salary (2026 federal, state and local taxes), home values and cost to own, jobs by industry (HVAC, electrical, engineering, utilities, tech, healthcare…), days over 90°F, terrain, scenery, education, health and more.

Everything is in one self-contained `index.html` (data embedded). Sources and caveats are listed at the bottom of the page.
